A skeleton's frames say what each signal's body may do, and `soma check` warns with W0019 when the skeleton says more than the program does: a frame wider than its body, a machine edge or a slot no frame touches, or a helper nothing calls. Here `level` is granted `writes stock` but its body only reads the slot, so the frame overstates what the signal can change. Narrow the frame to what the body does: `reads stock`.
