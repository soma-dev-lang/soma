What the declarations `entry`, `internal` and `held` show on the frames, before any body exists: an `internal` signal that no signal of the program has in its `calls`; an `entry` that takes an entity the law guards and neither `realizes` an action of the law on it nor calls a signal that does (a second path); a signal that writes a source of a `held` slot without its holder in its `calls` (the held slot goes stale); a second writer of a held slot. `soma check` reports these as warnings, never as E0145, and counts them on its last line (`fidelity not judged: …`).
