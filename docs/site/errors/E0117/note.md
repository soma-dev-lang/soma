`await approve(…)`, `await signal(…)` and `await sleep(…)` are durable waits: the handler stops, what it wrote so far commits, and the rest runs when the answer arrives, even after a restart. Only a `[task]` handler is made of such steps, so `soma check` reports E0117 for an `await` in any other handler, and also for an `await` that is not a whole statement of the handler's body or of an `if` branch (inside a loop, or inside a larger expression). Mark the handler `[task]` after its parameter list: `on refund(id: String, amount: Int) [task] { … }`.
